Compensation & Market Reality

SDR / BDR

Base $55–75K · OTE $75–110K

High-volume outbound. Look for clear meeting-to-opportunity conversion and whether the AE team is actually closing.

Mid-Market AE

Base $90–140K · OTE $180–280K

The sweet spot. Quota realism, ramp time, and manager quality are the real differentiators.

Enterprise AE

Base $140–180K · OTE $280–450K+

Named accounts, longer cycles. Top performers clear $500K+. Equity and accelerators matter most here.

Sales Leadership

VP Sales OTE $260–450K+

Stage, team size, and whether the motion is repeatable determine the real package and equity.
